Who We Are

DefenseNet Solutions is a cybersecurity services firm founded with a singular purpose: to make enterprise-grade security accessible to every organisation, regardless of size or budget. We are headquartered in Thalassery, Kannur, Kerala, and serve clients across India and the Gulf region.

Our team consists of certified security professionals with expertise spanning Security Operations, Penetration Testing, Incident Response, Cloud Security, and Compliance. We combine deep technical knowledge with a practical, business-focused approach — ensuring that the security solutions we deliver are not just technically sound, but genuinely useful to the organisations we protect.

Whether you are a growing startup, an established enterprise, or a government body, we tailor our services to your specific threat landscape, regulatory environment, and operational requirements.

What We Do

Security Operations Centre (SOC)

24/7 managed threat monitoring, detection, and response using SIEM, EDR, and threat intelligence platforms.

VAPT / Penetration Testing

Comprehensive vulnerability assessments and penetration testing for networks, applications, APIs, and cloud environments.

Incident Response

Rapid containment, investigation, and recovery from cyber incidents including ransomware, data breaches, and insider threats.

Cloud Security

Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM), IAM reviews, and continuous compliance monitoring for AWS, Azure, and GCP.

SIEM Implementation

Deployment, tuning, and ongoing management of SIEM platforms (Splunk, IBM QRadar, Microsoft Sentinel) for enterprise visibility.

Security Consulting & Compliance

Frameworks and compliance advisory: ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, NIST CSF, SOC 2, and India's DPDPA 2023.
