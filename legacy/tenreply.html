What is Tenreply?

Tenreply is a WhatsApp Business API platform developed and operated by DefenseNet Solutions, a cybersecurity company headquartered in Thalassery, Kannur, Kerala, India. The product is available at tenreply.com.

Businesses use Tenreply to connect with their customers on WhatsApp at scale — sending order updates, appointment reminders, promotional messages, OTPs, and managing two-way support conversations through a simple, unified dashboard.

Built with the same commitment to security and reliability that defines all DefenseNet Solutions products, Tenreply integrates with Meta's official WhatsApp Business API to ensure compliant, high-throughput messaging.

Tenreply Features

Everything your business needs to communicate on WhatsApp at scale

WhatsApp Business API

Powered by Meta's official WhatsApp Business API — send and receive messages at enterprise scale with full API compliance.

Automated Messaging

Set up automated flows for order confirmations, OTPs, appointment reminders, and marketing broadcasts triggered by your business events.

Two-Way Conversations

Manage inbound customer replies through a unified inbox — support teams can respond, route, and resolve chats from one dashboard.

Bulk Broadcast

Reach thousands of opted-in customers simultaneously with personalised broadcast messages — promotional, transactional, or informational.

API & Webhook Integration

Secure & Compliant

How It Works

1
Register Your Business

Sign up on tenreply.com and complete your WhatsApp Business profile with your business name, logo, and description.

2
Connect Your Number

Link your business phone number to the WhatsApp Business API through our streamlined Meta verification process.

3
Create Message Templates

Design and submit message templates for Meta approval — for transactional alerts, OTPs, and marketing messages.

4
Start Messaging

Send messages via dashboard or API, manage replies in the inbox, and track delivery and read receipts in real time.
