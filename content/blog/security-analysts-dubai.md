Every Dubai CISO we speak to has the same problem: they need experienced security analysts, the market is short of them, and the good ones are hired away quickly. There are two broad ways to solve it — hire directly, or bring in analysts from a specialist provider. Most organisations end up using both.

## Hiring in-house

**Strengths:** analysts who belong to your organisation, build deep knowledge of your business and stay aligned with your culture and priorities.

**Challenges in the UAE market:**

- **Time to hire.** Finding, interviewing, relocating and onboarding an experienced analyst can take months.
- **Cost beyond salary.** Visa, housing allowances, training, certifications and tooling add up.
- **Continuity.** Leave, illness and resignations leave gaps, and 24/7 coverage needs several people per seat.
- **Skill breadth.** One or two analysts cannot be expert in SIEM, EDR, cloud, threat hunting and incident response at the same time.

## Security analyst as a service

With this model, a provider supplies analysts who work inside your tools and processes — full-time or shared, on-site or remote — while the provider handles recruitment, training, supervision and replacement.

**Strengths:**

- Analysts working in weeks rather than months.
- L1 triage through L3 investigation and threat hunting, matched to the shifts you need.
- Continuity built in: when someone is on leave, cover is the provider's responsibility.
- Access to specialist depth — incident responders, detection engineers — when needed.

**Things to get right in the contract:**

1. Named analysts and the right to interview them.
2. Clear escalation boundaries and decision rights.
3. Confidentiality, background checks and data handling terms.
4. Conversion terms if you later want to hire someone permanently.
5. Measurable KPIs: queue handling, escalation quality and improvements delivered.

## The hybrid most teams settle on

A common pattern in Dubai is a small internal security team that owns strategy, architecture and stakeholder relationships, with embedded analysts handling monitoring and investigation — and a managed SOC covering nights, weekends and holidays.
