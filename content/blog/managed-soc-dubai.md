Most attacks on UAE businesses do not happen during office hours. Ransomware operators favour weekends and holidays — Eid, National Day, the Saturday–Sunday weekend the UAE adopted in 2022 — because they know fewer people are watching. A managed security operations centre (SOC) exists to close that gap: someone qualified is watching your environment every hour of every day, and acts when something goes wrong.

## What a managed SOC actually does

A managed SOC collects security telemetry from across your organisation — endpoints, servers, firewalls, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, cloud accounts and identity systems — and analyses it continuously. When something suspicious appears, an analyst investigates, decides whether it is a real threat, and either contains it or escalates it to you with clear next steps.

The value is not the dashboard. It is the decision. A good SOC turns thousands of daily events into a handful of investigated incidents, each with an explanation of what happened and what to do.

## Why it matters specifically in the UAE

- **Regulation expects monitoring.** The UAE's Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) requires appropriate technical measures to protect personal data and to notify the UAE Data Office of breaches that affect privacy. You cannot report what you never detected. Dubai Government entities and their suppliers working under the Dubai Electronic Security Center's (DESC) Information Security Regulation face explicit monitoring and incident-handling expectations.
- **The region is targeted.** Gulf organisations have been hit by destructive attacks for more than a decade — see our [Shamoon case study](/resources/incidents/saudi-aramco-shamoon-2012/) — alongside the same ransomware, business email compromise and credential theft seen worldwide.
- **Edge devices are the front door.** VPNs and firewalls from Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, Citrix and Ivanti are common in Dubai, and all have had mass-exploited vulnerabilities in recent years. Monitoring them is as important as patching them.

## In-house, outsourced or hybrid?

Running a 24/7 SOC internally means staffing every hour of the year. Once leave, training and attrition are counted, covering one seat around the clock takes roughly five people — before you buy any tooling. For most Dubai SMEs and mid-sized enterprises, a managed SOC is the only realistic way to get round-the-clock coverage.

Larger organisations often choose a hybrid: an internal team during the working week and a partner covering nights, weekends and overflow. We cover that model in our [SOC build and modernisation](/services/soc-build-modernisation/) service.

## Questions to ask a managed SOC provider in Dubai

1. **Who looks at my alerts at 3 AM on a Saturday?** Ask for the staffing model, not the marketing line.
2. **What do you do, versus what do you tell me to do?** Agree containment authority in writing — isolating a laptop, disabling an account, blocking an IP.
3. **Which of my systems will you actually monitor?** Endpoint, identity, email, cloud and firewall coverage should be listed explicitly.
4. **Where is my data stored?** If you need logs kept in the UAE, confirm the provider can do it.
5. **How will you prove value?** Expect monthly reporting on incidents, response times and detection coverage against MITRE ATT&CK.
