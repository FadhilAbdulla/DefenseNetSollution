Most Dubai businesses already have something installed on their laptops that calls itself security. The question is whether it would stop a modern ransomware operator — and whether anyone would notice if it did not.

## Antivirus, EDR and XDR

- **Traditional antivirus** compares files against known-bad signatures. It is useful, but attackers increasingly avoid malware altogether, using legitimate tools such as PowerShell, remote management software and stolen credentials.
- **EDR (endpoint detection and response)** records behaviour — processes, network connections, registry and file changes — and detects suspicious patterns even when no malicious file is involved. Crucially, it can **respond**: isolate a machine, kill a process, roll back changes.
- **XDR (extended detection and response)** joins endpoint data with identity, email and cloud signals, so a phishing email, a suspicious sign-in and an unusual process are seen as one incident.

## The part most organisations miss

EDR raises alerts. Someone has to read them. Ransomware is commonly deployed at night or over a weekend, and the window between the first warning signs and mass encryption can be short. An EDR console that is checked on Monday morning is a forensic record, not protection.

## Common gaps we find in UAE environments

1. **Coverage gaps.** Servers, contractors' laptops or recently purchased machines without the agent installed.
2. **Prevention switched off.** Policies left in "detect only" mode because a business application once triggered an alert.
3. **Broad exclusions.** Entire folders or applications excluded from scanning — exactly where attackers hide tools.
4. **No tamper protection.** Attackers with admin rights simply uninstall the agent.
5. **Unlicensed capability.** Many organisations on Microsoft 365 E5 or Business Premium already own Defender for Endpoint but have not configured it.

## Choosing a platform

CrowdStrike, Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, SentinelOne, Sophos and Trend Micro are all capable. The bigger differences are in how well the product is configured and operated. Before buying, check what you already own and what your team — or your provider — can run well.
